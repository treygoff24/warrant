The three milestones

M0 · exact inputs and a testable foundation

Workspace, gate with CI on stable and MSRV, core nouns and schemas, snapshot (what exactly are we looking at), inventory (every file classified or listed as unowned), the CLI shell, the conformance and corpus stages, the six-repository corpus pin. Closes with a Fable-run critical review of the whole diff.

M1 · a useful map before decisions

Program model, TypeScript and Cargo integrations, policy language, declared facts in the model, query, census, context, propose, map, the Claude Code hook. Three spikes measure what we only believe today: compiler references (S1, then your D10 decision applied), parity with tsc (S6), retrieval usefulness (S7, which must pass for M1 to close). Two of your approvals sit in the path by design.

M2 · honest advisory checks, then R14

Obligations, patterns, evidence binding with real report predicates, signals, the four-facet verdict with its receipt, signing you confirm before ssh-keygen runs, the widening classifier, the receipt verifier, mutation testing, adversarial fixtures. Then the Atlas dogfood, a Fable code review of the whole M2 diff, an S7 rerun on that exact build, your approval of the frozen reference policy, and the early comparison: benefit, mixed, or no benefit.

How one task flows

1 · Astra buildsOne lane, owned files only, verify rows must pass. Retry slot is Sol xhigh if Astra stalls.
2 · Three reviewsOpus, Grok, GLM in parallel from a frozen checkout; adversarial seats use isolated worktrees.
3 · Fix roundAdopted majors go back to the same Astra lane. Standard: one round. Critical: two.
4 · Scoped re-reviewOnly the changed surface is re-read. Disputes go to the Opus adjudicator.
5 · Integrate and verifyLuna merges, Luna verifies, full gate runs at HEAD before anything closes.

What the experiment records per task: major findings on the first review round, fix rounds consumed, and the route that closed it. Compared against prior Luna-executor runs on this estate, stated as an observation, not a controlled result.

Your five decisions inside the plan

  1. W1.D10 (after S1): adopt a compiler-reference surface as a pinned instrument, or stay at binding level with the affected contracts labeled.
  2. W1.C1 (after census on Atlas): read and amend the Atlas module and ownership declarations. S7 queries run against what you approve here.
  3. W1.C2: freeze the S7 sample, the failure-cost criteria, and the output budget before the held-out measurement, so a result cannot be relabeled a pass later.
  4. W2.C1: approve the full Atlas reference policy, the comparison rubric, and what counts as useful, after the M2 build is reviewed and requalified.
  5. W2.G = R14: benefit, mixed, or no benefit. Proceed to M3, rethink the guidance, or rule on a split result.

Not inside the plan, carried forward by name: D2 permanent identifiers before durable signing, the D5 deployment choice and its authorization, S5 on macOS, the deferred adversarial rows, and everything M3 to M5.
